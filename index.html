03Case files

Four tangles I’ve already untangled.

Client work is anonymised: no names, no identifying numbers. The diagrams are redrawn, not screenshots. Drag each slider from before to after, or keep scrolling and watch them resolve.

Case 01 · Finance and reporting

A verified finance command center for a multi-company group

The tangle
Several sets of company books on several office PCs. Statements and schedules assembled by hand in spreadsheets, and GST matched manually.
What I did
Built a strictly read-only bridge to the accounting software: nothing is ever written back. Each company syncs month by month, with change detection, into its own verified mirror database.
The untangled system
A live dashboard with KPIs, ledgers, ageing and audit checks. GST reconciliation against the government’s auto-drafted returns. A Telegram assistant for cash, overdues and P&L, with large-voucher alerts and a morning digest. And an AI assistant with 36 read-only tools for drill-down questions.
Result
One verified, read-only view of the whole group’s books, from any phone, without touching the source of truth.

Case 02 · Public-sector workflow

A government billing workflow, redesigned from the data up

The tangle
A monthly, multi-district approval chain. Vendors typed totals and uploaded the same figures again as spreadsheets. Officers printed, signed and scanned. In the legacy data, bills took a median of 3.1 days to reach final approval, 7% came back for resubmission, and none of the scanned signatures could be verified.
What I did
Audited the legacy system and its data, then designed the replacement: one monthly upload per vendor, every line reconciled automatically against official point-of-sale records, and approve-and-sign-all with a single one-time PIN.
The untangled system
Bills are generated, not typed. Clean bills go straight to a single HQ approval. Finance receives a payment-order export with service-level deductions already applied.
Result
Designed to cut about 15 manual touches per bill to 3, and to make every signature verifiable. Design target

Case 03 · Export and market entry

A UK and Europe entry blueprint for an Indian commodities exporter

The tangle
A good product, a big ambition and a hundred open questions: which markets, which products, through whom, and at what margin once freight and duty are paid.
What I did
Researched the market and wrote the entry blueprint, built the economics report and a commodities master sheet, and structured the partnership as a business-unit proposal, with separate versions for the principal and for a distributor.
The untangled system
One decision-ready pack, and a meeting deck to open the conversation with UK partners. The documents are built from scripts, not hand-formatted, so every revision stays consistent.
Result
A clear answer to “what do we sell, through whom, at what margin”, and the deck to walk into the room with.

Case 04 · ERP

One ERP for a two-entity, multi-state Indian group

The tangle
Two legal entities, a branch for every state registration, and payroll rules that change every time the law does. Spread across sheets, inboxes and memory.
What I did
Designed the architecture on open-source Odoo, multi-company from day one, and orchestrated the build. Every customisation lives in its own module, never in the core, and production is never touched during development.
The untangled system
Statutory values such as PF, ESI, professional tax and TDS stored as dated data records instead of code, so a rate change is an entry, not a rewrite. Tiered approvals, intercompany flows and a mobile-first interface.
Result
A group that runs on one system, with payroll and approvals driven by data, not hard-coded rules.

04Method

Trace. Untangle. Hand over.

  1. 01 Trace

    I map the process as it really runs, not as the manual says. Every hand-off, every spreadsheet, every “I just WhatsApp him”.

  2. 02 Untangle

    Cut the steps that add nothing, automate the ones that repeat, and add checks so the numbers can be proven, not just believed.

  3. 03 Hand over

    Documented, tested and owned by your team. You should not need me to keep it running, and you will know exactly how it works.
